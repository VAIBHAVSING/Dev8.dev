## ❓ FAQ

### Can I change plans anytime?
Yes! Upgrade instantly. Downgrades take effect next billing cycle. No cancellation fees.

### What happens if I exceed my compute hours?
Professional: $0.08/hour overage charges
Professional+: $0.07/hour overage charges
Enterprise: No limits

### How do AI API costs work?
You can:
1. Use included credits (Professional+/Enterprise)
2. Bring your own API keys (all tiers with AI access)
3. Buy credit packs as needed

### Is there a free trial?
Yes! 14-day free trial of Professional+ tier. No credit card required.

### What about data privacy?
- Your code never leaves your environment
- We don't log API calls to AI providers
- See our [Privacy Policy] for details

### Can I use my own Docker images?
- Community: Yes (BYO infrastructure)
- Professional/Professional+: No (managed images only)
- Enterprise: Yes (custom configurations available)
